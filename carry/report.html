China Futures Carry Recurrence Report

Reproduction paper: Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen, Vrugt, "Carry",Journal of Financial Economics, 2018 · Commodity samples 2006-01 to 2026-09; stock index and treasury bonds are extended

The carry effect is very significant on Chinese commodity futures and is stronger than the overseas sample of the paper: cross-section carry1-12 paper-style specification Sharpe 0.91, after executing according to the realistic execution assumptions, there will still be 0.71, retaining about 78%; almost uncorrelated with temporal momentum (0.07).

The time series carry of the stock index is meaningless: CSI 500 and CSI 1000 futures are at a discount all year round (the average carry value is about +10% and +8%), which mainly comes from the hedging demand of quantitative products rather than dividends. "Carry > 0 long" is almost always long, which is equal to holding the stock index. Therefore, the cross-sectional version is used for all three categories during synthesis.

There is almost no correlation between the three categories (monthly correlation: commodity-stock index -0.14, commodity-treasury bond 0.04, stock index-treasury bond -0.02). After synthesis, the Sharpe is significantly improved, which is consistent with the conclusion of the paper GCF Sharpe 1.20. However, during the same period, there were only about 6 years, stock indexes and government bonds each had only 2~3 tradable instruments, and using full sample fluctuation scaling (same paper), conclusions need to be cautious.

Conclusion

  1. Carry is significant and robust on Chinese commodity futures: The paper-style backtest has a Sharpe ratio of 0.91 (t = 4.2), versus 0.71 under realistic execution assumptions; there is no clear decay after 2016.
  2. carry1-12 is better than carry1m: The term structure of Chinese commodities has strong seasonality. After removing the seasonality on the 12 monthly average, the signal is cleaner and the turnover is lower.
  3. Hardly related to TSMOM(0.07), the dispersion effect of the combination of the two will be very good.
  4. Not sensitive to transaction time, small loss of realistic-execution backtest: retains about 80% paper-style specification Sharpe, while TSMOM retains only about half.
  5. The price is a crisis retracement: The financial crisis in 2008 retraced approximately 32%, consistent with the carry crash risk described in the paper.
  6. Multi-asset synthesis is effective: The carry of commodities, stock indexes and treasury bonds are not correlated with each other. After synthesis, the Sharpe increases, but the financial futures sample is short and the instrument is small.

Limitations and next steps

  • The near and far months are selected based on open interest, which is different from the paper's selection based on expiry date; the paper's interpolation method is difficult to use directly under China's 1/5/9 contract structure.
  • In the early days (years 2006~2009), there were only 10~15 instruments per month, and the cross-sectional ranking was relatively coarse.
  • The real cost model is the same as TSMOM, and the impact coefficients are still empirical values.
  • Next step: Carry + TSMOM combination; the three factors of commodity cross-section (Bakshi, etc.) will directly use the carry factor here.
