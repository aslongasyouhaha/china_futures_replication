Basis-Momentum Recurrence Report

Reproduction paper: Martijn Boons & Melissa Porras Prado，《Basis-Momentum》，Journal of Finance, 2019 · Chinese commodity futures, 2007-01 to 2026-08, 221 months, 69 instruments · Month-end settlement price

According to the paper method, basis-momentum is on Chinese commodity futuresCan predict returns, but is not an independent factor. The long-short return of univariate sorting is annualized 13.1% (t = 3.16), and the paper is 18.4% (t = 6.73); but it highly overlaps with ordinary momentum: after controlling the Bakshi-Gao-Rossi three factors (including momentum), α is only 7.3% (t = 1.71), the pooled regression controlling for instrument and time fixed effects is also not significant (t = 1.88, paper 6.81). The conclusion of the paper is that basis-momentum absorbs momentum, and China does the opposite. When calculated using the most liquid contracts in China (dominant contract and sub-dominant contract), the basis-momentum is stronger and is still significant after controlling for three factors (α 9.6%, t = 2.42), and the spread income is particularly robust.

What does the paper talk about

Basis-momentum = rolling return of the front-month contract over the past 12 months − rolling return of the second front-month contract over the past 12 months. It measures changes in the shape of the term structure: it can be broken down into two components: average curvature (how much steeper the near end is than the far end) and changes in slope. The paper uses 1960–2014 years to find 21 American commodities: sorted by basis-momentum, the highest 4 is reduced to the lowest 4, the annualized return of the nearby contracts is 18.4%, the spread return (nearby contracts − the second nearby contracts) 4.1%, Sharpe Duyo 0.9; It is stronger than basis (carry) and momentum, and basis and momentum are no longer significant in joint regression; it has a large α for the existing commodity three-factor model; the paper believes that it compensates for the risks borne by speculators and intermediaries when volatility is high and liquidity is poor, rather than inventory or hedging pressure.

Conclusion

  • Phenomenon exists: The long-short portfolio sorted by basis-momentum makes significant money in China, with both front-month and spread return directions consistent with the paper.
  • According to the paper method, it is not a new factor: In China, it highly overlaps with ordinary momentum, and is no longer significant after controlling momentum; the core conclusion of the paper (BM absorbs momentum and basis) does not hold, and the direction is opposite.
  • Change to China’s dominant contract caliber, which is more like an independent factor, especially the spread income. This shows that term structure information in China must be obtained from active contracts.
  • relationship with previously discovered: In the three-factor recurrence, we find that the predictive power of momentum mainly comes from the past basis. BM is a measure of "past basis change", but in China it highly overlaps with momentum and past average basis, and none of them is significant under instrument fixed effects. In other words, these signals of Chinese products are mainly about distinguishing instruments, not timing.
