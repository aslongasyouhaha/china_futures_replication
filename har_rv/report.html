China Futures HAR-RV Recurrence Report

Reproduction paper: Fulvio Corsi, "A Simple Approximate Long-Memory Model of Realized Volatility",Journal of Financial Econometrics, 2009 · 8 instruments, sample to 2026-09

The conclusion of the paper is established on Chinese futures: the HAR model using only daily, weekly, and monthly average volatility has a prediction effect that is only 0% to 3% different from the long memory model ARFIMA, which is much better than the EWMA currently used by TSMOM (the prediction error for the next 22 days is 18% lower). The persistence of futures volatility in China is stronger than overseas.

The long memory of Chinese futures is real and stronger than overseas

The estimated d ranges from 0.45 to 0.65, partially exceeding the stationary boundary of 0.5 (about 0.4 in overseas literature). In order to test whether this is a "pseudo long memory" caused by structural changes such as the launch of the night market, the following comparison was made:

  • After eliminating the known steps, d remains basically unchanged (mostly within ±0.05).
  • For the 10-year Treasury bond, which has no structural changes, d is the highest (0.64).
  • d does not show the typical rules of pseudo-long memory under different bandwidths.

Conclusion: The high persistence is real and is not caused by known changes such as night trading; it may be related to the fluctuation phase formed by policies and macro cycles that lasts for months to years (speculation, not tested). ARFIMA's d is truncated at 0.49, which actually suppresses the true persistence; HAR does not need to assume that d, the coefficient sum is less than 1, and is always stationary.

What it means to TSMOM

Position in TSMOM = 40% ÷ Forecast Volatility. Replacing EWMA with HAR or ARFIMA, the monthly volatility forecast can be accurate to about 18%~21%, which is expected to reduce positions in a more timely manner when volatility amplifies. Whether it can increase the Sharpe or reduce the retracement, it needs to be put in research/time_series_momentum The real version of the backtest test; it should be noted that HAR predicts the volatility during the trading period, excluding overnight gaps, and must be converted to an all-day caliber when placed in TSMOM.

Differences from the paper

  • Substitute 5-minute returns for TSRV on tick data.
  • ARFIMA's d is estimated using Local Whittle rolling and truncated at 0.49; the paper uses the full sample estimation.
  • Multi-step forecasting: HAR uses direct regression and ARFIMA uses daily recursion.
  • Only 8 major instruments are covered; the intraday volatility noise of unpopular instruments is greater.
