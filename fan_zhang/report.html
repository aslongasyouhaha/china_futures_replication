China Commodity Futures Risk Premium Reappearance Report

Reproduction paper: John Hua Fan & Tingxi Zhang，《The Untold Story of Commodity Futures in China》，Journal of Futures Markets, 2020 · China Commodity Futures No. 1~4 nearby contract, 2005-01 to 2026-08 (the paper sample period is 2004-02~2017-05, and then out-of-sample)

The main conclusions of the paper are in China and in the 9 years after the paper.Basically established: Term structure and momentum are the only solid premiums, and out-of-sample is still significant (momentum No. 1 nearby-contract annualized 24.9%, t = 4.81; term structure No. 2 nearby-contract 12.8%, t = 3.40); hedging pressure, position changes, and liquidity are not significant in both periods; there is no premium for simply going long (the sample period of the paper). The difference: the volatility premium reverses out-of-sample; the value becomes significantly negative out-of-sample (1st nearby-contract -16.2%, t = -3.21); the skewness turns positive out-of-sample. In addition, the direction of the "hedging pressure" we constructed according to the paper's definition is opposite to that of the paper; the exchange rate is not significant (same as the paper), and inflation is significant and disappears out-of-sample during the paper's sample period.

What does the paper talk about

Chinese commodity futures are dominated by retail investors, with strict position limits and margin increases as the contract approaches expiration. The nearby contract trading volume is less than 10% of the entire curve. The paper used 30 instruments from 2004 to 2017 to test ten types of premiums on the 1st to 4th nearby contracts, and found that: simply going long does not make money; the term structure and momentum premium are robust (and "amplified" by position limits and margins); hedging pressure, volatility, liquidity, and skewness premium are "distorted" by the system; position changes, exchange rates, and inflation premiums are sensitive to the institutional environment.

Comparison with thesis

  • consistent reproducibility: There is no premium for simply going long; momentum is significant in all terms; term structure is significant in the 2nd to 4th nearby-contract; position changes and liquidity are not significant; the volatility premium of the 1st nearby-contract is significantly positive and the skewness is negative.
  • inconsistent: The first nearby-contract term-structure signal is weaker than in the paper (6% versus 16%), while the second nearby-contract signal is strong (19%). The first nearby contract is often inactive in China, making the first-versus-second-nearby spread noisy. The samples also start at different dates: 2004-02 in the paper and 2005-01 here. Hedging pressure (|ΔOI|/volume) is negative at all maturities, versus a significant positive 8.1% for the paper’s third nearby contract. The paper itself describes this as a crude proxy.
  • The exchange rate is insignificant in each period, which is consistent with the paper (but our exchange rate data only covers the last 27 months of the paper's sample period). Inflation is significantly positive in the 1st nearby-contract (10.5%, t 2.35), which is stronger than the paper (2.2%), which may be related to the simplified inflation proxy we use; out-of-sample disappears.

9 years after thesis (out-of-sample)

  • Momentum and term structure still hold: The momentum is stronger in the 1st and 2nd nearby-contracts, and the term structure is still significant in the 2nd nearby-contract. This is consistent with other recurring findings in this library: the solid premium for Chinese commodities is carry and momentum.
  • Volatility Premium Reversal: The first nearby contract is significantly positive during the paper sample and negative afterward. The paper attributes this to nearby-contract position limits, but that explanation does not hold out of sample.
  • The value is significantly negative: Instrument, which has fallen much for a long time after 2017, continues to fall (1st nearby-contract t ≈ −3.2), which is consistent with the "Chinese commodity value factor is negative" in the AMP recurrence of this library.
  • Skewness turns positive: The 3rd and 4th nearby-contracts make money out-of-sample by going long to the left and short to the right (t ≈ 2), which is consistent with the "skewness premium turning positive after 2016" in the recurrence of skewness in this library.
